📋 How to connect your free Google Sheet (3 mins)
  1. Create a new spreadsheet in Google Sheets named FilterFlow Data.
  2. Share the spreadsheet with your wife's Google account so you both have access.
  3. Click Extensions > Apps Script.
  4. Replace all code in Code.gs with the script from google_sheets_sync_script.js in this project.
  5. Click Deploy > New deployment:
    • Type: Web app
    • Execute as: Me
    • Who has access: Anyone
  6. Authorize permissions and copy the Web App URL into the field above!
